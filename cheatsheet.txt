===============================================================================
  ComputerCraft (CC:Tweaked) Character Set Cheat Sheet
===============================================================================

This file shows the characters available in the default ComputerCraft terminal.
You can produce any of these characters in your Lua scripts using `string.char(code)`.

NOTE: Characters 0-31 are non-printable control characters and may not display
      correctly in a standard text editor. They are represented here by their
      common Unicode symbols for reference, but in CC:Tweaked they are blank.
